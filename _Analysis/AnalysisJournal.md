## 10.15.26 | Journal Pass

_"I think it's important to note that the real impetus here is just to think about time and games in a thoughtful way, and this initial idea may end up not being the way forward. But, a good place to start the exploration I think."_ [02.19.26](https://github.com/mouseandthebillionaire/lautir/blob/main/_Analysis/Data/Journal.md#021926--initial-idea)

That does a pretty good job of capturing the big picture. Time + Games. And then specifically how does _limited_ and _forcibly structured_ time effect a play experience?

On February 24 there's a big breakthrough. This idea of alignment comes in conversation with Z, and that ends up being a big touchstone of the whole project. Specifically the idea of alignments within nature and the built environment. Something that I didn't make the connection with at the time, but is clear to me now is how those alignments contribute/foster/encourage ritual. Daily rituals. The happy hour. Waking up at a certain time to do yoga as the light streams in (and how that practice _changes_ over the year as the sun placement differs). The Weekly rituals. TGIF. Getting bakery treats after school on a Thursday. Yearly holidays aligning with moons, and suns, and seasons. The holiday party. The birthday dinner. 

I had immediately forgotten both "numinous" and "apophenia" as useful terms, but seeing them again sparks something. Apophenia is probably a bit more bleak, and numinous might be _too_ spiritual, but still good concepts to be dwelling on a bit more.

Very nice to see some design decisions arise from prototyping. On May 26 I mention that, while working in the Max patch, I had an idea to switch the song from a linear presentation to a looping one. This also introduces the "letter as instrument" paradigm. It is noteworthy that this looping standard doesn't _exactly_ stick around, but it is one contributing phase to what's to come. 

On May 27 I did a bunch of visual prototypes after a meeting with PB that I never did anything with. Looking at them again (with 20/20 hindsight) should I have moved for3ward with some combination of these. The radio reveal isn't great (and brings to mind JJ Abrams notion of the [Mystery Box](https://www.ted.com/talks/j_j_abrams_the_mystery_box)), but is there some version of it that's better? Does it work better with a better line drawing? My gut reaction now is that the photograph resolving is the best, and maybe including it in the back of the circles would be nice. Though I think it would be good if it never _fully_ resolves; it's a bit odd when it crisps up.
