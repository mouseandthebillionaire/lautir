LAUTIR is a daily experience that explores time, presence, and ritual.

Enter one five-letter word between 12 and 12:10pm each day and be presented with a unique piece of music.

The sonic parameters are informed by the letter choices you have made.

Each day builds on the last.

The song builds itself up, plays for 16 bars, and then removes word-instruments as it returns to its oroginal state, giving you time to reflect.

Come every day for five days to experience a full song composed of your words.
