## Bio

As Mouse & the Billionaire, **M Bethancourt** develops experimental games and interactions. He is interested in contemplative play, alternative controllers, procedural sound, sonification, and probably spends too much time thinking about negative space and deep time. Much of his work invites people to do silly or mundane things, which points to deeper themes and often poses unanswerable questions. He has an MFA in Design & Technology from Parsons School for Design in New York and is currently working towards a PhD at Concordia University in Montreal, QC.

## Intent And Thematic Connection

For LAUTIR, I was interested in creating a small game that fosters a daily playful ritual. Over five days, players enter a word of their choice at a specific time of day. That word is then turned into a unique sonified composition based on their letter choices, with the song being added to over the five days, creating a daily playfully contemplative experience.

## Tools And Process

LAUTIR was built in Unity for WebGL, with procedural sound composed and generated through Max/RNBO. The process moved through timed-access prototypes, visual alignment studies, and word-to-music sketches before settling on a five-word song structure shaped by repeated daily input.

## Artist Statement

LAUTIR began as a question about what happens when a game refuses constant availability. Over five days, players have a short daily window to enter a word, then hear those words converted into a layered procedural song, fostering a playfully contemplative experience.
